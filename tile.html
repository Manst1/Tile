Crafting

Bundles of Wheat, huts and other buildings: press Place in your inventory, then click a green-outlined tile. Every building takes one slot: Plains have 3 slots, Pens 2, every other tile 1. Bundles feed a Pen's animals for 60s each, and the timer shows all its bundles combined. A Small Hut houses 2 people, a Straw Hut 1. Add people with the ＋ buttons at the top (never more than your housing, and they can never leave). Press D to delete buildings (nothing is refunded) and S to rebuild a tile. Once people move in, they need Water, Food and Clothing: craft Bread (5 Wheat) and Clothing (1 Wool + 1 Leather makes 5), then drag items onto their Shared supplies.
